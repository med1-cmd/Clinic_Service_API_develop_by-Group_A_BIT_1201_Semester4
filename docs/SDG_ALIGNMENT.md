# Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) Alignment

## Clinic Service API MVP - SDG 3: Good Health and Well-Being

### Overview
The Clinic Service API MVP aligns with **UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 (SDG 3)** by supporting the delivery of safer, more accessible healthcare services through digital workflows.

### MVP Assumptions
- The current implementation targets essential healthcare workflows, including clinic search, appointment booking, medical records, and prescriptions.
- The MVP prioritizes secure access, role-based permissions, and usability before adding advanced features.

### How the API Supports SDG 3

#### 1. Improved Healthcare Access
- Digital appointment booking allows patients to connect with clinics without unnecessary travel
- Clinic and doctor search endpoints help patients find appropriate local services quickly
- Online access reduces friction for scheduling and managing care

#### 2. Better Clinical Information Management
- Centralized medical records store patient history, diagnosis, and treatment details
- Doctors and patients can access the right information securely
- Improved record accuracy reduces the risk of misdiagnosis and duplicate procedures

#### 3. Safer Prescription Management
- Prescriptions are created and tracked in the system, reducing manual errors
- Patients can view their prescriptions through authenticated access
- Doctors maintain control over medication updates and refills

#### 4. Efficient Appointment Workflows
- Appointment lifecycle tracking supports pending, approved, completed, and cancelled states
- Doctors and patients can manage appointments more transparently
- Administrative views support better clinic resource planning

### Key Features Supporting SDG 3

| Feature | SDG 3 Benefit |
|---------|---------------|
| Clinic search and discovery | Easier access to local healthcare services |
| Secure authentication and RBAC | Protects patient privacy and trust |
| Medical records management | Supports continuity of care |
| Appointment scheduling | Reduces delays and improves access |
| Prescription handling | Promotes safer medication use |
| Dashboard reporting | Supports data-driven healthcare decisions |
| Async notifications | Enables better patient communication |

### Alignment with SDG 3 Targets

- **Target 3.4**: Reduce premature mortality from non-communicable diseases through improved care management
- **Target 3.8**: Achieve universal health coverage by strengthening digital health infrastructure
- **Target 3.b**: Support access to essential medicines and health technologies through prescription tracking

### Conclusion
The Clinic Service API contributes to SDG 3 by improving access, security, and reliability in healthcare management, especially for appointment handling, patient records, and prescription workflows.
